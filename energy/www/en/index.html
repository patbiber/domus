Feed-in from 2027

When feeding in suddenly costs money

From 2027 in Switzerland, unless agreed otherwise with the grid operator, solar power is paid hour by hour at the spot market price. The minimum remuneration remains – but only as a top-up paid after each quarter on every kWh fed in. On sunny days the market price drops below zero around midday, and as soon as it falls below that top-up, you pay to feed in. homi keeps recalculating the threshold for your system – using the official federal reference market prices. That makes a big difference: in 2025, feeding in with the minimum remuneration would really have cost money in only 36 hours, not in the 303 hours with a negative market price. And a battery that simply charges as soon as the sun shines is long full by the time it would pay off most.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need a solar system?

No. Even without solar, homi shows what electricity costs you and when, and finds power guzzlers. It gets really exciting with a solar system, battery, heat pump or EV.

Which devices are supported?

Live today: Fronius inverters with a smart meter. I connect other inverters, batteries, wallboxes and heat pumps via open standards – list your devices in the form and I'll check them.

How do I reach my homi?

Under your own address like sonnenhof.homi.solar, in the browser or as an app on your phone. Sign in with a code by email or with Face ID / fingerprint. See an example

Where is my data stored?

At your home or on servers in Switzerland – not with an international cloud provider. No tracking, no sharing with third parties.

Does homi control my devices?

Yes, step by step. The feed-in stop as soon as feeding in costs money (from 2027 calculated with the minimum remuneration and quarterly top-up) already runs with Fronius inverters (via Modbus, locally in the house), plus an on/off button in the app and in Home Assistant. homi switches on appliances on smart plugs (e.g. myStrom) as soon as there is solar power to spare – such as the charging station for phone and tablet. Battery, wallbox and heat pump come next. Every control starts in test mode, you decide when to activate it – and every command expires by itself should homi ever fail.
